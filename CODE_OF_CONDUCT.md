# Code of Conduct

Participation in `revoq` should be professional, respectful, and focused on the
technical work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Assume good intent while asking for clear evidence and reasoning.
- Keep feedback specific to code, design, documentation, benchmarks, or project
  process.
- Respect maintainer decisions about scope and project direction.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruption of technical discussion after maintainers ask for it to
  stop.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when
needed to keep the project usable for contributors.
